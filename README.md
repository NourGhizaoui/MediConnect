🏥 MedicoConnect

Smart Medical Appointment Booking Platform | Plateforme intelligente de prise de rendez-vous médicaux


📌 Project Overview

MedicoConnect is an academic software engineering project focused on designing an intelligent web platform for medical appointment booking and healthcare appointment management.

The project addresses common challenges in the healthcare sector, including difficulty finding available doctors, time-consuming appointment scheduling, and communication between patients, doctors, and medical secretaries.

MedicoConnect aims to simplify access to medical consultations through online booking, integrated payments, automated notifications, and an AI-powered chatbot.

🎯 Project Objectives

Help patients find available doctors based on specialty, location, and availability.

Simplify online medical appointment booking.

Support online consultation payments.

Automate appointment confirmations, reminders, and cancellation notifications.

Provide dedicated workspaces for doctors, secretaries, and administrators.

Integrate an AI chatbot to guide patients in finding doctors and planning appointments.

✨ Main Features

👤 Patient Space

Account registration and authentication.

Search for doctors by name, medical specialty, location, and availability.

Book medical appointments online.

Pay consultation fees online.

View appointment history.

Receive appointment confirmations, reminders, and cancellation notifications.

Modify or cancel appointments according to booking rules.

Interact with an AI chatbot for assistance with doctor searches and appointment planning.

🩺 Doctor Space

Access a professional dashboard.

View confirmed appointments.

Manage availability and time slots.

Access patient profiles and medical history, subject to authorization.

Add consultation notes.

Manage professional information, including specialty, qualifications, fees, and location.

Monitor activity and statistics through a dashboard.

🧑‍💼 Medical Secretary Space

Access an appointment management dashboard.

View appointments for doctors within the medical practice.

Confirm, reject, or modify appointments.

Manage doctors' schedules and availability.

Notify patients of appointment status changes.

Create appointments manually on behalf of patients.

🛡️ Administrator Space

Manage patient, doctor, and secretary accounts.

Add, update, and delete medical specialties.

Monitor overall platform activity, statistics, and performance.

🤖 AI Chatbot

Guide patients through the doctor search process.

Help users identify relevant medical specialties.

Assist with appointment planning and booking navigation.

The chatbot is intended to support appointment navigation, not to replace professional medical diagnosis.

🏗️ System Requirements

Performance

Target response time of less than two seconds for search and booking operations.

Design target of at least 200 simultaneous connected users without significant performance degradation.

Security

Secure password storage using hashing algorithms such as bcrypt.

Role-based access control for patients, doctors, secretaries, and administrators.

Protection of sensitive patient information through appropriate access restrictions.
